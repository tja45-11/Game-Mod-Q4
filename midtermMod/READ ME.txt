Quake Impact is a Quake 4 mod based on Genshin Impact. The mod adds elemental effects to weapons, and by switching weapons the player can perform elemental reactions which will increase the player's damage.
Anemo + Pyro/Hydro/Cryo -> Swirl (Flat damage increase + reapplies first element)
Pyro + Hydro -> Vaporize (Damage multiplied depending on first element applied) (1.5 for Hydro, 2 for Pyro)
Pyro + Cryo -> Melt (Damage multiplied depending on first element applied) (1.5 for Pyro, 2 For Cryo)
Cryo + Hydro -> Freeze (Percent of max health damage for first freeze + applies cryo, reapplies cryo for future hydro applications)
Geo + Pyro/Hydro/Cryo -> Crystalize (Flat damage increase then multiplied by 1.2)
By defeating enemies, the player can obtain gear which can switch the element of their weapons. Open the menu to do so by pressing B.
Default elements:
Blaster: Hydro
Macchinegun: Hydro
Shotgun: Cryo
Hyperblaster: Pyro
Grenade Launcher: Anemo
Nailgun: Geo
Rocket Launcher: Physical
Railgun: Geo
Lightning Gun: Pyro
Dark Matter Gun: Anemo
